B G S Corner General Trading L.L.C. ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

We may collect the following types of personal data:

We collect information:

We use your personal data only for purposes necessary to provide our services and improve your experience:

* To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.
4. Legal Basis for Processing
We process your personal data based on:
* Contractual necessity: to perform a contract with you (e.g., delivering products).
* Legitimate interests: to improve our business, secure our website, and provide effective customer service.
* Legal obligation: to meet regulatory requirements under UAE law.
* Consent: for optional activities like marketing newsletters (you may withdraw consent at any time).
