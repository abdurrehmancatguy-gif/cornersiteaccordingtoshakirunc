01 — Who We Are

Leading general traders with
kiosk specialisation in the UAE.

BGS Corner sources, finishes and places goods across categories — and builds the retail floor they sell from. Kiosks are our specialisation: we take a unit from drawing to fit-out, joinery to lighting to vitrine.

Right now that capability is pointed at fragrance. Concentrated attar and oud, set in cut crystal, finished in 24K-tone metal, and built around two things most bottles do not carry: a bar of fine gold you can take out, and a diamond seated inside the cap.

03 — Our Products

A bottle that holds
gold, a diamond, and one tola.

The concept is simple. The attar is the contents; the bottle is the asset. A 2.5 g bar of 999.9 fine gold sits behind the front plaque on a drawer you can open. A 1 ct diamond sits in the cap, under a dome that lifts.

2.5 g fine gold
  1. 01

    A gold bar in the front plaque

    A 2.5 g BGS bar in 24K fine gold, 999.9, visible through a transparent scratch-resistant window and held in a black PVD cartridge.

  2. 02

    It comes out on a drawer

    Press the hidden notch at the top of the frame. Magnetic assist pops the mini drawer out two to three millimetres; pull the cartridge, take the bar, slide it back, and the safety latch locks it in.

  3. 03

    A diamond inside the cap

    The globe cap opens on a magnetic lock to a diamond seated in a soft-lined cup, held by a transparent retaining ring. Lift it, look at it, seat it back.

  4. 04

    Twelve millilitres of attar

    One tola of concentrated attar in a cut-crystal body, framed in a 24K finish — the measure the oud trade has always used.
