01 · Who We Are

Leading general traders with
kiosk specialisation in the UAE.

BGS Corner is a general trading company. We source across categories, handle the import and the paperwork, hold and distribute stock, and supply trade buyers at wholesale. Kiosks are our specialisation: we take a unit from drawing to handover, joinery to lighting to vitrine.

Right now that capability is pointed at fragrance: concentrated oud in cut crystal, cased to be opened in front of someone. The category is what the market asks for; the trade behind it is the constant.

Four lines of work.

    Sourcing, supply and distribution across categories, handled from order through to shelf.

  2. ii

    Our specialisation. Retail units designed, built and fitted: joinery, lighting, vitrines, display and stock.

  3. iii

How we trade

The work behind the goods.

Whatever the category, the job is the same: find it, land it, hold it, move it, and put it somewhere it sells.

  1. Sourcing & Supply

    Finding the goods, vetting the supplier and agreeing the specification before anything is committed.

  2. Import & Export

    Documentation, clearance and freight in and out of the U.A.E.

  3. Wholesale

    Bulk supply to retailers, distributors and trade buyers, priced by volume.

  4. Private Label

    Your name on the product: composition, bottle, packaging and artwork.

  5. Retail Fit Out

    Kiosks and counters taken from drawing through joinery, lighting and vitrine to handover.

  6. Gifting

    Cased sets and corporate volumes, built to be handed over rather than shelved.
