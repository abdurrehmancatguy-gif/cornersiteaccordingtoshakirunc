01 · General Trade

Leading general traders with
kiosk specialisation in the UAE.

BGS Corner is a general trading company based in Deira, Dubai. We source across categories, handle the import and the paperwork, hold and distribute stock, and supply retailers and trade buyers at wholesale.

What moves through us is whatever the market is asking for: vapes and pods, mobile accessories, audio, bags, eyewear, souvenirs, gifting and fragrance. The categories change with demand. The way we buy, land and place them does not.

How we trade

The work behind the goods.

Whatever the category, the job is the same: find it, land it, hold it, move it, and put it somewhere it sells.

  1. Sourcing & Supply

    Finding the goods, vetting the supplier and agreeing the specification before anything is committed.

  2. Import & Export

    Documentation, clearance and freight in and out of the U.A.E.

  3. Wholesale

    Bulk supply to retailers, distributors and trade buyers, priced by volume.

  4. Distribution

    Holding stock, breaking bulk and moving it to counters across the Emirates.

  5. Private Label

    Your name on the product: specification, packaging and artwork.

  6. Gifting

    Cased sets and corporate volumes, built to be handed over rather than shelved.

02 · Kiosks

Our specialisation.

A kiosk is a shop with no walls to hide behind. It has one chance to stop someone crossing an atrium, so every part of it has to work: the sightline, the lighting, the height of the counter, what sits at eye level.

01 Design
  1. 01

    Design

    We draw the unit to the pitch it will stand on: footprint, sightlines, circulation, where the queue forms and where the till goes. Mall landlords get the drawings they need to approve it.

  2. 02

    Build

    Joinery, lacquer, banding and glass, built off site and delivered as sections so the fit happens overnight rather than over a week.

  3. 03

    Fit

    Power, lighting, lit vitrines, security and signage. Lighting is the part most units get wrong; it is the part that makes stock look worth buying.

  4. 04

    Stock

    We can fill it too. Vapes, accessories, souvenirs and gifting come from our own supply, replenished from our stock rather than reordered from abroad each time.

  • FormatsIsland, in line, counter and pop up
  • DeliveredDrawing, build, fit out, handover
  • PlacedMalls, hotels, airports and high street
  • StockedFrom our own supply, replenished locally
