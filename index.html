01 — Who We Are

Leading general traders with
kiosk specialisation in the UAE.

BGS Corner sources, finishes and places goods across categories — and builds the retail floor they sell from. Kiosks are our specialisation: we take a unit from drawing to fit-out, joinery to lighting to vitrine.

Right now that capability is pointed at fragrance. Concentrated attar and oud, set in cut crystal and finished in polished metal — six forms, one measure, and a case built to be opened in front of someone.

03 — Our Products

Cut crystal,
and one tola of attar.

One measure, six forms. Each bottle is cut crystal with a faceted stopper and a polished collar, holding twelve millilitres — one tola — of concentrated attar.
